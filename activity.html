What Are Math Competitions?

Math competitions are opportunities for students to apply mathematical thinking and problem-solving in a competitive environment. They range from school-level contests to international championships. Learn more about their significance at MAA Math Competitions.

Popular Math Competitions

Some of the most renowned competitions include:

  • The International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), which is the premier global math competition.
  • The American Mathematics Competitions (AMC), a stepping stone to advanced math challenges.
  • The Art of Problem Solving (AoPS), which offers a wealth of resources and competitions for all levels.

How to Prepare

Success in math competitions requires preparation and strategy. Consider the following tips:

  • Practice regularly with past papers from Past Papers Net.
  • Join online forums such as the AoPS Community to discuss challenging problems.
  • Use resources from Brilliant.org to build problem-solving skills.

Join a Math Club

Joining a math club or group can help you stay motivated and connect with like-minded individuals. Many schools and universities have active clubs, or you can find online communities such as MathCounts.
