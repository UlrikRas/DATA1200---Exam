Accessibility for OsloMet

Our Commitment

We are committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. If you encounter any issues, please let us know.

Standards

Our website strives to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard Navigation: Navigate our site without a mouse.
  • Alt Text: Images include descriptive alt text.
  • High Contrast Mode: Colors ensure readability.

Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any issues, please contact us:

  • Email: itservicedesk@oslomet.no
  • Feedback Form: Submit feedback

Limitations

We are continually working to improve our accessibility. Some content may not yet meet standards, and we welcome your input to address these gaps.
